# StelligenOS Capabilities

Capabilities are stable OS-level abilities.

## Initial Capabilities

- Opportunity Discovery
- Knowledge Mining
- Rule Learning
- Evidence Extraction
- ADC Design
- Binder Engineering
- Patent Analysis
- Due Diligence
- Portfolio Management

## Notes

- Capabilities are not lifecycle stages.
- Capabilities are not core objects.
- Capability-specific prompts may evolve frequently without changing the architecture contract.
