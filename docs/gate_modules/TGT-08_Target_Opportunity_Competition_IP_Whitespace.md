## The two things MOD-TGT08 must never fuse

> **TGT-08 evaluates the external opportunity landscape; it does not evaluate
> the target's scientific validity and it does not decide whether this sponsor
> should proceed.**

> **IP whitespace is an evidence-backed landscape signal. It is not freedom to
> operate.**

Three distinct layers must stay separate:

| Layer | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Scientific Gates TGT-01…07** | target biology / ADC feasibility / normal-tissue liability |
| **TGT-08** | the external opportunity landscape — competition, target-specific differentiation, IP-whitespace **signals** |
| **Sponsor axis** (v5 §7) | should *this* sponsor invest, how, and where — `OUT_OF_MANDATE` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` are **not** a `KILL` |

`NEGATIVE` is **reachable** on TGT-08 (unlike TGT-05, a one-way liability
detector). A complete, audited opportunity landscape can weigh *against* a
differentiated entry. But that `NEGATIVE` means only *"current public
opportunity evidence weighs against a differentiated entry for this target in
refractory mCRC"* — it is **not** a scientifically bad target, a `KILL`,
`STOP_FOR_SPONSOR`, `OUT_OF_MANDATE`, an FTO-blocked finding, or *"no viable
molecule can be designed"*. And a favorable TGT-08 never de-risks TGT-01…07.

## Gate ordering

Fatal-first + cheap-first: **TGT-01 → TGT-05 → TGT-08 → TGT-02 → TGT-03 →
TGT-04 → TGT-06 → TGT-07**. TGT-01 calibrated the Module mechanics; TGT-05 is
the first fatal-first target-liability pruning Gate; **TGT-08 is the commercial
/ strategic opportunity gate and the first Gate whose canonical Assessment can
be `NEGATIVE`** — which is why its semantics are frozen *before* any code: it
slides very easily into the old failure mode of blending a scientific, a legal
and a sponsor judgement into one score.

## The 17-item acceptance checklist

| # | Item | What MOD-TGT08 must satisfy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gate identity & version** | `TGT-08@1.0`, `ADC_TARGET_GATESET@1.0`, L04, candidate ownership `ADC_TARGET`, bound to `INST-CRC-REFRACTORY-ADC-TARGET-v1`. Inherited, not owned. |
| 2 | **Primary Module identity & version** | `MOD-TGT08` (deterministic: `MOD-<GATE without hyphen>`). Contract v`0.1.0`; implementation version `0.0.0` = declared, not built. PR E6 builds it and raises the version to ≥ 1.0; **PR E5 does not change the binding version.** |
| 3 | **Gate question** | Quoted **verbatim** from the frozen PR D contract: *"Is there a differentiated opportunity for this target in refractory mCRC: unmet need, competitive landscape, and IP whitespace signals?"*. Not widened, not narrowed. It does **not** answer: the target's scientific validity, a freedom-to-operate opinion, whether a design-around exists, or whether this sponsor should invest. |
| 4 | **Admissible evidence classes** | The union of the frozen ladder classes: primary-source competitive / clinical landscape (trial registries / regulatory / company disclosures) **together with** a composition-level patent-landscape review; pipeline-database competitor summaries; a target-level (not composition-level) patent search; opportunity asserted from indication-level unmet need alone. **Not** admissible here: TGT-01/02/03/04/05/06/07 evidence; a freedom-to-operate / infringement / validity / design-around conclusion (not admissible into any L04 Gate); sponsor capability / cash runway / risk appetite / company mandate (sponsor axis, not Gate evidence). Derived-parity tested against `evidence_required` + the ladder. |
| 5 | **Evidence Ladder & ceiling** | Reproduced verbatim from PR D (parity-tested). `DIRECT` = current competitive / clinical landscape for the target in mCRC from trial registries / regulatory / company disclosures, **together with** a composition-level patent-landscape review. `INDIRECT_STRONG` = pipeline-database competitor summaries / a target-level (not composition-level) patent search. `WEAK` = opportunity asserted from indication-level unmet need alone. Ceiling: *primary-source competitive and regulatory landscape for the target in mCRC plus a composition-level patent-landscape review.* No new rungs, no exceeding the ceiling. |
| 6 | **Direction interpretation** | Strength ⟂ Direction; never a numeric or ranking score. Direction is about the evidence *relative to the Gate question* (a differentiated opportunity), not sponsor desirability. The Module places each atomic fact into `SUPPORTS_OPPORTUNITY` / `OPPOSES_OPPORTUNITY` / `CONTEXTUAL`; the provider supplies facts only. **Frozen truth table (no E6 discretion):** on a completed axis — only material target-specific opportunity-supporting signals → `POSITIVE`; only material opposing signals → `NEGATIVE`; both → `CONFLICTING`; at `DIRECT` if both axes complete, else `INDIRECT_STRONG`. Indication-level unmet-need-only → `INCONCLUSIVE / WEAK` (never *"grim indication ⇒ good opportunity"*), and it does **not** participate in a target-specific `CONFLICTING` (strong target congestion + huge unmet need is `NEGATIVE` at the strong rung, not `CONFLICTING`). Materially incomplete landscape → `INCONCLUSIVE / UNKNOWN`; **never** `UNKNOWN` → attractive / uncrowded / whitespace, and **never** a favorable commercial picture → TGT-01…07 de-risked. Sponsor capability / cash runway / risk appetite / mandate **must not** enter Direction. |
| 7 | **Allowed / forbidden inference** | Verbatim from PR D (parity-tested). Allowed: *the relative attractiveness and crowding of the target opportunity*; *IP whitespace signals for the target and ADC compositions (dense claim coverage or apparent congestion feeds a `NEGATIVE` target-opportunity assessment, not a `KILL`)*. Forbidden: *scientific de-risking (TGT-01 through TGT-07) from a favorable commercial picture*; *a freedom-to-operate opinion or a "no viable design-around" conclusion (a composition-level FTO judgement is not possible at L04, before any epitope / antibody / linker / payload / DAR is chosen)*. Also forbidden for the Module: infringement / validity / enforceability / legal-clearance judgements; a *"this molecule will infringe"* or *"no design-around exists"* conclusion; a *"no differentiation path"* conclusion; converting a sponsor / mandate / capital constraint into a Gate Direction. |
| 8 | **Fatal conditions** | Verbatim from PR D (parity-tested): *a dominant, well-protected competitor ADC already approved or in registrational trials for the same target in mCRC with no differentiation path* — **a commercial / strategic potential fatal signal decided on the sponsor axis (v5 §7), not a canonical scientific `KILL`**. The machine may detect a **candidate pattern** when: same target + ADC modality + same refractory mCRC context + approved OR in a registrational trial + primary-source verified (not a pipeline-database row alone) + composition-level patent congestion evidence present. The machine **never** asserts the competitor is *dominant*, the estate is *well protected*, there is *no differentiation path*, or this sponsor should stop — all human / sponsor-governance reserved. The machine emits **at most** a module-local `sponsor_review` record with `status = POTENTIAL_SPONSOR_FATAL_PATTERN` — **never** a canonical fatal flag / `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE`. *"No differentiation path"* cannot be derived by MOD-TGT08 — future differentiation may come from a novel epitope, a different antibody, conditional binding, the linker / payload, DAR, the bystander profile, patient selection, a combination or a regimen, none chosen at L04. |
| 9 | **Evidence-source plan** | Frozen regime `PUBLIC_PRIMARY`; `INST-CRC-REFRACTORY-ADC-TARGET-v1` is `PUBLIC_ONLY` → PR E5 designs the current public path only, **no experiments** (TGT-08 has none — gaps go to public search, external legal review, or sponsor governance). **Two axes.** *Competitive-landscape axis* — direct authority: trial registries, regulatory filings / approvals, company primary disclosures, primary clinical publications where needed; must cover approved / registrational / active clinical / discontinued-failed programs against the target and disclosed reasons, same-target ADC and relevant same-target non-ADC targeted programs, and refractory / mCRC indication relevance for each; pipeline databases are a discovery / inventory / reconciliation index only → at most `INDIRECT_STRONG`. *Composition-level patent axis* — direct authority: actual patent publications / families / legal-status records; must produce relevant composition claim families, assignees, family / legal status, declared jurisdiction / search scope, claim-category mapping, apparent congestion / whitespace signals; a target-level (not composition-level) patent search caps at `INDIRECT_STRONG`; Lens / PATENTSCOPE / Google Patents / EPO-class tools may discover / search / link families / normalize metadata, but the canonical provenance of a claim fact is the actual patent publication / official status source (index ≠ evidence authority — same rule as ADCdb in TGT-01); a composition-level patent landscape is **not** an FTO judgement. **Absence inference needs completion provenance:** a favorable *"no competition found"* signal is admissible only from a **complete audited search** returning no qualifying competitor — never from `records == []`; *"no patent found"* is not *"patent whitespace"*. The future Module carries module-local typed completion state (conceptually a `CompetitiveLandscapeCompletion` and a `PatentLandscapeCompletion`), each pinning `as_of` date, search scope, sources searched, context / target, unresolved items and a completion status — run-level machine records, **not a seventh core object**. **No universal threshold** — no competitor / patent-family / claim count cutoff, no ranking score. **PR E5 connects no provider.** Retrieval / entity-resolution / provenance / serialization are shared infrastructure (§6.5). |
| 10 | **Input contract** | `candidate_id` + the candidate's **canonical target identity** (single authoritative target — the MOD-TGT01 / PR E2 gene; no separate drift-prone target argument); `instantiation_id` + context; the frozen `gate_contract_ref` / `evidence_ladder_ref`; the refractory mCRC context; run context (`run_id`, `evidence_regime = PUBLIC_ONLY`); **`landscape_as_of`** (TGT-08 is time-dependent — a landscape with no as_of date is not admissible); the retrieval / patent **search scope + declared jurisdictions**; existing `evidence_refs`. **No implicit default context** — fail rather than assume. |
| 11 | **EvidencePackage output contract** | Atomic and **Gate-neutral** — one neutral landscape fact, no grade / direction, **no TGT-08 opportunity conclusion stamped on it**; full `provenance` from the resolved canonical `SourceIndex` record; `candidate_refs`; `measurement` / `claim`; observation-level `interpretation_boundary`; `derivation`. A competitive observation states e.g. *"PROGRAM_X is in a registrational trial in mCRC against this target"*; a patent observation states e.g. *"PATENT_FAMILY_X contains a composition claim of category Y with legal status Z"*. A package **never** carries *"TGT-08 NEGATIVE patent evidence"*, *"FTO blocked"*, *"bad target"*, *"no differentiation path"* or any Gate-relative conclusion. Reference an existing EP by `evidence_id` and reuse the **exact** canonical package — never copy / re-create (PR C). On reuse, every classification-driving `study_context` field must be **present AND equal**; a missing or drifted field is a **HARD identity integrity failure** (PR E2 / E4 gene). Forward `status` / `superseded_by` never on `evidence.json`. |
| 12 | **Assessment proposal envelope contract** | The Module emits a **non-canonical, module-local proposal envelope** — *not* a `CandidateGateAssessment`. Carries the **canonical assessment identity pins** (`candidate_id`, `instantiation_id`, `context_id` / `context_version`, `gateset_id` / `gateset_version`, `gate_id` / `gate_version`) plus `proposed_direction` + `proposed_strength` (`POSITIVE` / `NEGATIVE` / `CONFLICTING` / `INCONCLUSIVE` × `DIRECT` / `INDIRECT_STRONG` / `WEAK` / `UNKNOWN`; never a Decision / score), `evidence_refs` `[{evidence_id, role}]`, `aggregation_rationale`, `critical_unknowns`, `evidence_ceiling`, and the machine acceptance record. **Omits** `assessment_id`, `assessment_version`, the `review` block, any FTO / infringement / validity conclusion, any *"no differentiation path"* conclusion, a fatal flag / `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE`, and a numeric / ranking score. The structured potential-sponsor-fatal-pattern signal lives in a separate **module-local `sponsor_review` record on the run result** (a distinct record from TGT-05's `fatal_review`, routed to sponsor governance not the scientific `fatal_gate_policy`; not a proposal field, not a `CandidateGateAssessment` field, not a new core object, not a Decision): `required` / `status` (`POTENTIAL_SPONSOR_FATAL_PATTERN`) / `evidence_ids` / `competitor_program_ids` / `patent_family_ids` / `landscape_as_of` / `patent_scope`. `required = true` iff a same-target ADC program is approved OR in a registrational trial in refractory mCRC, primary-source verified, with composition-level patent congestion evidence present. The review surface constructs the canonical `CandidateGateAssessment` after `HUMAN_APPROVED` (item 14). PR E5 only defines the field lists — no new core object, no runtime code. |
| 13 | **Machine acceptance criteria** | Every EP validates; every `source_id` resolves to a canonical `SourceIndex` record whose metadata matches, and every `evidence_id` resolves (PR C / PR E2 / E4 genes); every evidence class is in the item-4 admissible list and none in `not_admissible`; strength ≤ the highest rung actually met (pipeline-DB or target-level-patent evidence alone caps at `INDIRECT_STRONG`); `proposed_direction × proposed_strength` is exactly the item-6 truth-table result; the **item-16 two-axis mandatory completion** for the rung proposed is satisfied (`DIRECT` requires **both** the primary-source competitive axis and the composition-level patent review complete); any absence-based whitespace / no-competitor claim is backed by an explicit **completed-search provenance record** — never `records == []`; if `sponsor_review.required = true`, its status is `POTENTIAL_SPONSOR_FATAL_PATTERN` (never a canonical fatal flag / `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE`) with the contributing `competitor_program_ids` / `patent_family_ids` / `landscape_as_of` / `patent_scope`; dedup; **no numeric / ranking score anywhere**; **no FTO / infringement / validity / "no design-around" / "no differentiation path" wording anywhere**; **no TGT-01…07 scientific inference and no sponsor Decision / `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE` anywhere**. On failure → rejected with a machine reason. A **HARD identity / provenance integrity failure** (target misbinding, missing / mismatched canonical source, incompatible canonical EP, an absence claim with no completion provenance) rejects the **whole run** — never degraded to an accepted `UNKNOWN`; `UNKNOWN` from a genuinely incomplete landscape is *not* an integrity failure (PR E2 / E4 gene). |
| 14 | **Human acceptance / review surface** | The human sees: proposed direction + strength + rationale; each EP's claim / source / `interpretation_boundary`; the ladder rung per EP; the **two-axis completion state** (`CompetitiveLandscapeCompletion`, `PatentLandscapeCompletion`) with `as_of` date, search scope, sources searched, unresolved items; the **module-local `sponsor_review` record** (status `POTENTIAL_SPONSOR_FATAL_PATTERN`, the contributing `evidence_ids` / `competitor_program_ids` / `patent_family_ids` / `landscape_as_of` / `patent_scope`); `critical_unknowns`; the machine acceptance record. Human-only: whether a competitor's indication / context genuinely matches refractory mCRC; how to interpret a registrational-stage or discontinued status; whether the competition is strategically material; whether a patent family is genuinely relevant and whether apparent claim congestion is meaningful; whether the aggregation is reasonable; final `HUMAN_APPROVED` → the review surface constructs the canonical `CandidateGateAssessment`. **Sponsor-only:** whether the competitor is truly *dominant*, whether the patent estate is *well protected*, whether a credible *differentiation path* exists, and whether this sponsor proceeds. |
| 15 | **Failure / UNKNOWN / conflict behaviour** | Retrieval failure → a machine reason, not a partial assessment. Direction / strength follow the item-6 truth table: unmet-need-only → `INCONCLUSIVE / WEAK` (never *"good opportunity"*); a completed axis with only material target-specific opportunity-supporting signals → `POSITIVE`, only opposing → `NEGATIVE`, both → `CONFLICTING`, at the rung the completed evidence supports. Incomplete landscape → `INCONCLUSIVE / UNKNOWN` (*"Incomplete landscape → UNKNOWN"*); never converted to attractive / uncrowded / whitespace, and a materially incomplete axis cannot yield a target-opportunity judgement. A `NEGATIVE` target-opportunity assessment (including one driven by dense composition-level claim coverage) is a canonical Gate Assessment — **never** a `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE`, and it never de-risks or re-risks TGT-01…07. `critical_unknowns` use `PUBLIC_RESOLVABLE`, `CURRENTLY_UNRESOLVABLE`, or *"external legal / sponsor review required"* — TGT-08 does **not** use `EXPERIMENT_REQUIRED` (freedom to operate is not an experiment). `UNKNOWN` is never silently `PASS` / `HOLD` / `KILL`; `UNKNOWN` from an incomplete landscape is *not* an integrity failure; an absence-based whitespace claim **without** completion provenance **is**. |
| 16 | **Stop rule** | TGT-08 is time-dependent and two-axis — the stop is not *"found one competitor, stop"*. **Two-axis mandatory coverage:** before a complete landscape assessment, both the competitive-landscape coverage and the composition-level patent-landscape coverage must complete; **coverage complete ≠ `DIRECT` quality** (a complete pipeline-DB inventory + a complete target-level patent search can be coverage-complete but only `INDIRECT_STRONG` authority — it can still produce `POSITIVE` / `NEGATIVE` / `CONFLICTING` at `INDIRECT_STRONG`, not `UNKNOWN`); if one axis is not searched → the landscape is incomplete → `INCONCLUSIVE / UNKNOWN`. **`DIRECT` requires** the primary-source competitive axis complete **and** the composition-level patent review complete. **Sponsor-review provisional stop:** when the machine has found an approved / registrational same-target mCRC ADC together with a strong composition-level congestion pattern → set `sponsor_review.required = true`, `status = POTENTIAL_SPONSOR_FATAL_PATTERN`, **provisionally** stop chasing weaker unmet-need / secondary pipeline evidence, hand off to human / sponsor review — **but** the two core axes' completeness still must be satisfied (the competitor may not be dominant, the patent may be expired, key claims may not cover the ADC composition, another entrant may have failed, or a clear differentiation whitespace may exist). **Normal stop after mandatory coverage:** evidence ceiling reached; or additional public evidence cannot change Direction / Strength; or the enumerated public source space is exhausted; or the `sponsor_review` handoff is triggered after both core axes complete. A residual composition-level IP question is **never** `EXPERIMENT_REQUIRED` — it is `PUBLIC_RESOLVABLE`, `CURRENTLY_UNRESOLVABLE`, or routed to external legal / sponsor review. |
| 17 | **Downstream consumer / handoff** | The Module hands off `EvidencePackage`s + one **assessment proposal envelope** to the human review surface (item 14), and separately a module-local `sponsor_review` record to sponsor governance. Only **after** `HUMAN_APPROVED` does the review surface construct the canonical `CandidateGateAssessment`, and *that* record is consumed by: the `MatrixView` cell `(candidate_id, TGT-08)` (PR C); the `ADC_TARGET_GATESET` decision layer that turns the eight TGT assessments into a Candidate-level `Decision` (PR B); the next Gate (`TGT-02`) as context only. The `sponsor_review` record is routed to **external sponsor governance** (e.g. `SearchSpaceAdmission` / `ProgramCommitmentReview`) on the sponsor-relative axis (v5 §7). This Module does **not** construct a `CandidateGateAssessment` or emit a `HUMAN_APPROVED` record, produce a Decision / `KILL` / `STOP_FOR_SPONSOR` / `OUT_OF_MANDATE`, route the `sponsor_review` record through the scientific `fatal_gate_policy` or turn it into a canonical scientific fatal, make an FTO / infringement / validity / design-around conclusion, make a *"no differentiation path"* conclusion, de-risk or re-risk any of TGT-01…07 from a commercial picture, let a sponsor / mandate / capital variable enter the Gate Direction, write to another Gate's index or assessment, or modify the Matrix. |
